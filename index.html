About

AamirDev is the name I publish Android games under. I work alone on the code, the store listings and the support pages, so each project stays small enough to finish and maintain.

That means fewer promises and more finished work: a game that runs on ordinary phones, a listing that describes it accurately, and a privacy page that explains what the advertising SDK does.
