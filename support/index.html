Support

How to get help with an AamirDev game.

  • First, check for an update Open the Google Play Store, search for the game, and check whether an update is available. Most reported problems are fixed in a later build.
  • Skybound Adventures The game-specific support page explains what information to include with a report. Open Skybound Adventures support
  • Report a problem Send the game name, your device model, your Android version, and a short description of what happened through the contact page.
  • Privacy requests Data questions are handled under the privacy policy, which explains what is processed by the developer and what is processed by Google AdMob.

There is no ticketing counter or response-time guarantee shown on this site. Replies are sent as time allows, normally within a few days.
