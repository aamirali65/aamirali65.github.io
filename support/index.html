Help centre

Answers to the problems that come up most often with AamirDev games.

How do I update a game?

Updates are delivered through the Google Play Store. To check for one:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Manage apps and device.
  3. Open the Updates available tab and update the game if it is listed.

Most reported problems are fixed in a later build, so try updating before anything else.

A game closes as soon as I open it

Try these in order:

  1. Restart the device.
  2. Update the game from the Play Store.
  3. Clear the app cache: open Settings, then Apps, then the game, then Storage, then Clear cache.
  4. If it still crashes, reinstall the game. Local progress will be lost.

If none of that works, send a bug report using the details below.

Ads are not loading

Advertising needs a working connection. Check that mobile data or Wi-Fi is on, then reopen the game. If ads stay empty, the ad network may have no inventory for your region at that moment. This does not block play.

To limit personalized advertising, open Settings, then Google, then Ads, and reset or delete the advertising ID.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Google Play, not by AamirDev. Refunds are requested from Google:

  1. Open Google Play refund help.
  2. Follow the instructions for the purchase in question.

AamirDev cannot issue, approve or reverse a payment.

How do I report a bug?

Send a report through the contact page or the GitHub profile, and include:

  • The game name and version, shown in the app.
  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The steps that led to the problem, and what you expected to happen.

How do I delete my data?

The games have no account system and no developer-held player database. Local game data is removed when you clear the app storage or uninstall the app. Advertising data is held by Google and is managed through your Google account settings.

The privacy policy explains which data the developer handles and which data Google AdMob processes.

Where is the privacy policy for a specific game?

Each game has its own policy. For the current release, read the Skybound Adventures privacy policy.

No response time or support rating is advertised. Replies are sent as time allows.
