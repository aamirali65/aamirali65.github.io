Skybound Adventures help

Support for the Android game Skybound Adventures.

Update the game first

Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, then Manage apps and device, then Updates available. Fixes reach installed copies through normal store updates.

The game will not open

  1. Restart the device.
  2. Update the game from the Play Store.
  3. Clear the cache in Settings, then Apps, then Skybound Adventures, then Storage.
  4. Reinstall the game if the problem continues. Local progress will be lost.

Ads do not appear

Check the internet connection and reopen the game. Advertising depends on the ad network having inventory for your region, so an empty ad slot can happen normally and does not stop you playing.

Control personalized advertising

Open Settings, then Google, then Ads. From there you can delete or reset the advertising ID and turn off personalized ads. Ads will still appear, but they will not be selected using that identifier.

Report a bug

Send a report through the contact page with the game version, your device model, your Android version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Privacy

What the app and Google AdMob process is set out in the Skybound Adventures privacy policy.

No response time or support rating is advertised. Replies are sent as time allows.
