Skybound Adventures support

Help with the Android game Skybound Adventures.

  • Update the game Open the Google Play Store, go to your installed apps, and check for updates. Fixes are delivered through normal store updates.
  • Common issues If the game will not open, restart the device and clear the app cache in Android Settings. If ads do not load, check that the device has a working internet connection.
  • Advertising controls To limit personalized ads, open Android Settings, then Google, then Ads, and reset or delete the advertising ID. This does not remove ads from the game.
  • Report a bug Send the problem through the contact page and include: the game version shown in the app, your device model, your Android version, and the steps that led to the problem.
  • Privacy See the Skybound Adventures privacy policy for what the app and Google AdMob process.

No response time or support rating is advertised. Replies are sent as time allows.
