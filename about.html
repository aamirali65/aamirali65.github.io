About AamirDev

AamirDev is the name I publish Android games under.

How I work

I develop Android games and release them through the Google Play Store. The work covers the whole release: writing the game, testing it on physical devices, preparing the store listing, and keeping a public privacy policy and help page for each title.

I keep projects deliberately small. A smaller game can be finished, tested properly, and patched after release without promising features that never ship.

What a release includes

  • A public privacy policy, published on this site before the app goes live and linked from the Play Store listing.
  • A help page that explains updates, common problems and how to report a bug.
  • An honest listing. Descriptions and screenshots show the game as it is. There are no fabricated ratings, reviews or download figures anywhere on this site.

Development

Games are built for Android with attention to working controls, readable text on small screens, and predictable performance on mid-range hardware. Advertising, where used, is disclosed in the privacy policy for that game.

Contact

Questions go through the contact page. Privacy requests are covered by the privacy policy.
