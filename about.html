About AamirDev

AamirDev is the name I publish Android games under.

I develop mobile games for Android and release them through the Google Play Store. The work covers the full process: building the game, testing it on physical devices, preparing store assets, and keeping a public privacy policy and support page for every release.

I keep projects deliberately small. A smaller game can be finished, tested properly, and patched after release without promising features that never ship.

What to expect from a release

  • A public privacy policy The policy is published on this site before the app goes live and is linked from the Play Store listing.
  • A support route Each game has a support page that explains how to report a problem or ask a question.
  • Honest store listings Descriptions and screenshots describe the game as it actually is. There are no fabricated ratings, reviews or download figures.

Development

Games are built for Android with a focus on working controls, readable text on small screens, and predictable performance on mid-range hardware. Advertising, where used, is disclosed in the privacy policy for that game.

Contact

For questions, use the contact page. For privacy requests, see the privacy policy.
