What to include in a bug report

Include the game name, the game version shown in the app, your device model, your Android version, and the steps that led to the problem. That is usually enough to reproduce an issue.

Email is the fastest route for support and privacy requests. GitHub remains available for public issues, and there is no contact form that stores submissions.
