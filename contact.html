What to include in a bug report

Include the game name, the game version shown in the app, your device model, your Android version, and the steps that led to the problem. That is usually enough to reproduce an issue.

There is no published email address on this page, and no contact form that stores submissions. GitHub is the working contact method, and it is public without requiring an account to read.
