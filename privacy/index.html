Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Aamir Almani, located at github.com/aamirali65, regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our service and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

2. Identification

I am Aamir Almani, an independent Android game developer. My GitHub profile is at github.com/aamirali65. You can contact me via the contact form on my website or via email at aamirali65.github.io/contact.

3. Game Information

My website showcases Android games that I develop. These games are available on the Google Play Store. When you visit my website or play my games, certain information may be collected as described in this policy.

4. Google AdMob Integration

Yes, my games integrate Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob is a mobile advertising platform provided by Google that allows developers to display ads in their apps and generate revenue.

AdMob may collect and process the following types of data:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • Location information (approximate location based on IP address or GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, conversion tracking)
  • Cookie identifiers and advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID)
  • demographic information (age range, gender, interests based on activity)

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, which can be found at Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Personalized Advertising

I use AdMob to display advertisements in my games. These ads may be personalized based on the data described above. Personalized advertising means that ads are shown based on your interests, inferred demographics, and past activity.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings or by resetting your advertising ID. Please note that even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to your interests.

6. Device Identifiers and Advertising IDs

My games may access and transmit device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple's IDFA to AdMob for the purpose of ad targeting, frequency capping, and conversion tracking. These identifiers are resettable by the user in their device settings.

I do not store these identifiers on my servers. They are transmitted directly to AdMob for advertising purposes only.

7. Information Collected by Advertising SDKs

The following SDKs may be integrated into my games and may collect information:

  • Google AdMob: Collects advertising identifiers, device information, location data, and ad interaction data for the purpose of delivering and measuring ads.

These SDKs may automatically collect technical information about your device, including but not limited to: hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash reports.

8. Third-Party Services

In addition to AdMob, my games may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services - for game features and achievements
  • Google Analytics (if implemented) - for basic usage statistics (anonymous)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy that governs how they collect and process data. I encourage you to review their privacy policies for more information.

9. Data Sharing

I do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, I may share certain data with the following parties as described in this policy:

  • Google AdMob - for the purpose of displaying and measuring ads. The data shared includes advertising identifiers and device information as described in Section 4.
  • Google Play Store - when you download or interact with my games, certain information may be shared with Google as part of the normal Google Play Services functionality.

I do not share personally identifiable information with any third parties except as necessary for the functioning of AdMob and Google Play Services.

10. Data Security

I take the security of your data seriously. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While I strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, I cannot guarantee its absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

My games are directed to a general audience and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are concerned about your child's use of my games, you can control ad experiences through Google's Family Link or by resetting the advertising ID on the device.

12. Data Retention

I retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. AdMob may retain data according to Google's own retention policies.

13. User Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access - you can request a copy of the personal information I hold about you
  • Right to rectification - you can request that I correct any inaccurate personal information
  • Right to deletion - you can request that I delete your personal information
  • Right to object - you can object to the processing of your personal information
  • Right to data portability - you can request that I transfer your data to another controller

To exercise these rights, please contact me using the information provided in Section 14.

14. Contact Information

If you have any questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, you can contact me:

  • Email: aamirali65.github.io (use the contact form on my website)
  • GitHub: github.com/aamirali65

I will respond to your inquiries within 30 days.
