Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how AamirDev ("I", "me", "the developer") handles information in the Android games published under the AamirDev name and on this website. It is written to be read alongside the privacy policy of each individual game.

1. Developer identity

AamirDev is the publishing name used by the independent developer behind these Android games. The developer can be reached through the contact page on this website, at aamirali65.github.io/contact.html, or through GitHub at github.com/aamirali65.

2. Information the developer collects directly

The developer does not operate a user account system and does not ask players to submit a name, email address or phone number inside the games. The games do not include a sign-in.

If you send a message through the contact page, the information you choose to include in that message is used only to answer your question.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The games use Google AdMob to display advertising. AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google. The developer does not receive the individual advertising identifiers that AdMob processes, and does not build user profiles.

According to Google's published documentation, the AdMob SDK and the ads it serves may process the following categories of information:

  • Device information such as device model, operating system version and language settings.
  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, which the user can reset or delete in the device settings.
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Information about ad requests, impressions and interactions with an ad.
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information related to the ad SDK.

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller for its own advertising purposes. It is governed by Google's privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy, and by Google's information page on how it uses data from partner sites and apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Personalized and non-personalized advertising

Ads may be personalized, meaning they are selected using information associated with the advertising identifier and prior activity. Whether a request is personalized is determined by the advertising configuration, the region of the user, and the consent choices available in that region.

You can limit personalized advertising on Android by opening Settings, then Google, then Ads, and using the options to delete or reset the advertising ID and to opt out of personalized ads. Opting out does not remove advertising from the game, but ads are no longer selected using your advertising ID.

5. Consent where required

Where the law requires consent for personalized advertising or for the storage of information on a device, a consent prompt is presented before ads that require it are requested. Declining personalized advertising does not prevent you from using the game.

6. Analytics and other third-party services

The games do not include a separate developer-operated analytics service. Third-party software development kits included for advertising may collect the technical information described in section 3. The Android platform and the Google Play Store may separately collect installation and diagnostic information under Google's own policies, which are outside the developer's control.

If a future release adds a service not described here, this policy will be updated before that release is published.

7. Purposes of processing

  • To display advertising that supports the free distribution of the games.
  • To measure ad performance and prevent invalid or fraudulent ad activity.
  • To maintain the stability and security of the advertising software development kit.
  • To answer support and privacy questions that you send directly.

8. Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Information processed by the AdMob software development kit is transmitted to Google for the purposes described above. Information you send through the contact page is not shared with third parties except where necessary to answer your request or where required by law.

9. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS. The games do not store personal information on developer-controlled servers. Transmission to advertising services is carried out by the third-party SDK using its own transport security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Data retention

The developer does not maintain a database of player information, so there is no developer-held personal data set to retain. Messages sent through the contact page are kept only as long as needed to resolve the request. Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google's own retention rules.

11. Children's privacy

The games are not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a game is ever designated for a child audience on Google Play, its advertising configuration and this policy will be updated to match the Google Play Families policy before that designation is used.

Parents and guardians can control advertising personalization on a child's device through Google Family Link and the Android ads settings.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal information, or to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because the developer does not hold a player database, requests concerning advertising data processed by Google are best directed to Google using the controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Requests concerning information you sent directly can be sent through the contact page and will be answered within 30 days.

13. International transfers

Advertising data is processed by Google on infrastructure that may be located outside your country. Google states that it applies appropriate safeguards for these transfers in its privacy policy.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when a game changes, when a new service is added, or when the law requires it. The date at the top of this page shows the current version. Material changes are reflected here before the affected release is published.

15. Contact

For any question about this policy, use the contact page or the GitHub profile at github.com/aamirali65.
