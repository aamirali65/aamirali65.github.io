Privacy Policy for Skybound Adventures

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Developer Identification

This privacy policy applies to the Android game "Skybound Adventures" developed by Aamir Almani. I am an independent developer located at github.com/aamirali65. For any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact me via the contact form on my website at aamirali65.github.io.

2. Game Information

"Skybound Adventures" is an Android game available on the Google Play Store. The game provides aerial adventure gameplay with intuitive touch controls and sky-themed visuals. You can download the game from the Google Play Store at the provided link.

3. Google AdMob Integration

Yes, "Skybound Adventures" integrates Google AdMob for advertising purposes. AdMob is a mobile advertising platform provided by Google that allows developers to display ads in their Android applications.

Through AdMob, the following types of data may be collected:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Google Advertising ID (resettable identifier)
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)
  • Ad impressions and clicks
  • Conversion tracking data

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. I do not have access to the specific data that AdMob collects; all data collection and processing is handled by Google.

4. Advertising and Personalized Advertising

The game displays advertisements through AdMob. These advertisements may be personalized based on your interests and activity. Personalized advertising means that ads are shown based on your inferred demographics and past activity.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by:

  • Resetting your Advertising ID in your device settings
  • Opting out of Ads Settings in Google settings
  • Using the "Opt out of interest-based ads" option in your device

Please note that even if you opt out of personalized advertising, you will still see ads within the game, but they may be less relevant to your interests.

5. Device Identifiers and Advertising IDs

"Skybound Adventures" may access the Google Advertising ID (GAID) on your device for the purpose of ad targeting, frequency capping, and conversion tracking with AdMob. This identifier is resettable by the user in their device settings.

I do not store your Advertising ID on my servers or use it for any purpose other than ad-related functions through AdMob. The identifier is transmitted directly to Google's AdMob service.

6. Information Collected by Advertising SDKs

The primary SDK integrated into "Skybound Adventures" for advertising is Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may automatically collect the following technical information:

  • Hardware model of your device
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers (including Advertising ID)
  • Language preferences
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Internet connectivity status

This information is collected by the AdMob SDK and transmitted to Google for the purposes of ad targeting, delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

7. Third-Party Services

In addition to AdMob, the following third-party services may be used in "Skybound Adventures":

  • Google Play Services: May be required for certain game features, achievements, and leaderboards. Google Play Services may collect usage statistics and device information as part of its normal operation.

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing data collection and processing. I encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services.

8. Data Sharing

I do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, I may share data with the following parties as necessary for the functioning of the game and advertising:

  • Google AdMob - for the purpose of displaying and measuring ads. Data shared includes advertising identifiers and device information as described in this policy.
  • Google Play Store - when you download, update, or rate the game, certain information may be shared with Google as part of normal Google Play Services functionality.

I do not share personally identifiable information with any third parties except as necessary for the functioning of AdMob and Google Play Services.

9. Data Security

I take the security of your data seriously. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While I strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect personal information, I cannot guarantee its absolute security. AdMob follows Google's security practices for data handling.

10. Children's Privacy

"Skybound Adventures" is directed to a general audience and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The game does not target children specifically. If you are a parent or guardian and you are concerned about your child's use of the game, you can control ad experiences through Google's Family Link or by managing the Advertising ID on the device.

11. Data Retention

I retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. AdMob may retain data according to Google's own retention policies. Device identifiers and advertising data are typically retained by Google in accordance with their policies.

13. User Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access - you can request information about the personal data I hold about you
  • Right to correction - you can request that I correct any inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion - you can request that I delete your personal data
  • Right to object - you can object to the processing of your data for advertising purposes

To exercise these rights, please contact me using the information provided in Section 14.

14. Contact Information

If you have any questions or comments about this privacy policy, you can contact me:

  • Email: Please use the contact form on my website at aamirali65.github.io
  • GitHub: github.com/aamirali65
  • Game support: See the Support page on my website

I will respond to your inquiries within 30 days.
