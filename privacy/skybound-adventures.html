Skybound Adventures privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Skybound Adventures, published by AamirDev. It describes what the app does with information. It supplements the general AamirDev privacy policy, which also applies.

1. Developer and app identification

App name: Skybound Adventures.
Platform: Android, distributed through the Google Play Store.
Developer: AamirDev, independent developer.
Contact: aamirali65.github.io/contact.html
Email: contact.aamirali6565@gmail.com
GitHub: github.com/aamirali65

2. Information the app collects directly

Skybound Adventures does not require an account and does not ask for a name, email address, phone number or precise location. The developer does not operate a server that stores player information for this game.

Game progress is stored locally on the device. Uninstalling the app removes that local data.

3. Advertising through Google AdMob

Skybound Adventures uses Google AdMob to show advertising. AdMob is a third-party service provided by Google. The developer does not receive or store the individual advertising identifiers processed by AdMob.

According to Google's documentation for the AdMob SDK, the following information may be processed when ads are requested or displayed:

  • Device information, including device model, operating system version and language.
  • The Android Advertising ID, a resettable identifier. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings, under Google and then Ads.
  • Approximate location inferred from the IP address.
  • Ad request, impression and interaction events.
  • Diagnostic data related to the advertising SDK, such as crash information.

Google processes this information for advertising delivery, measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. Google's explanation of data use by partner apps is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Personalized advertising and consent

Advertisements may be personalized using the advertising identifier. Where consent is legally required, a consent prompt appears before personalized ads are requested. Declining personalized advertising does not block access to the game.

To limit personalized advertising on Android, open Settings, then Google, then Ads, and use the options to delete or reset the advertising ID or to opt out of personalized ads. Ads will still appear, but they will not be selected using that identifier.

5. Other third-party components

Aside from the advertising SDK, the app does not include a developer-operated analytics service. The Android platform and the Google Play Store may collect installation, crash and diagnostic information under Google's own policies. Those processes are outside the developer's control and are described in Google's privacy policy.

6. Permissions and network access

The app requests internet access so that advertisements can be loaded. It does not request access to contacts, the camera, the microphone or precise location. If a future version needs an additional permission, the store listing and this policy will be updated to explain why.

7. Sharing and sale of data

The developer does not sell personal information and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information processed by the advertising SDK is transmitted to Google for the advertising purposes described above. Information is disclosed otherwise only where required by law.

8. Data security

The app communicates with advertising services over HTTPS. Local game data stays on your device. No system is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data retention

The developer keeps no player database for this app, so there is no developer-held personal data to delete. Advertising data is retained by Google under Google's retention rules. Local game data remains on the device until the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

10. Children's privacy

Skybound Adventures is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app is ever enrolled in a child-directed program on Google Play, its advertising configuration and this policy will be updated to comply with the Google Play Families policy before that change takes effect.

Parents and guardians can manage advertising personalization on a child's device with Google Family Link and the Android ads settings.

11. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, to object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Requests about advertising data processed by Google should be sent to Google through myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. Requests about information you sent to the developer directly can be made through the contact page and will be answered within 30 days.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy is updated when the app's data practices change. The date at the top of the page reflects the current version. Any material change is published here before the affected app version is released.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page, by email at contact.aamirali6565@gmail.com, or on GitHub at github.com/aamirali65.
